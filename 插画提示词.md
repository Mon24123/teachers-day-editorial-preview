## 共用风格

Use case: illustration-story. Create an original editorial illustration for a Chinese Teachers Day eyewear campaign preview. Style reference is the attached montage ONLY for the visual language: very thin expressive midnight-navy pen contours on warm ivory paper, poetic restrained hand drawing, no photorealism, almost no shading, vast negative space, a few soft blush pink and pale sage watercolor strokes. NOT a copy of reference artwork. Elegant mature Chinese brand editorial aesthetic. No text, no letters, no numerals, no logos, no watermark, no typography. Crisp refined line work, natural anatomy.
